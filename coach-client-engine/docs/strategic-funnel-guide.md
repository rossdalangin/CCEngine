# 🌪 Your Strategic Funnel Guide: Building the Bridge to Profit

A funnel isn't just a series of pages. It’s a **psychological journey**. It moves a person from being a complete stranger to becoming a "Closed-Won" client who thanks you for taking their money.

The **Funnel Engine** turns complex marketing science into a simple "Paint-by-Numbers" system.

---

## 🚀 1. Launching Your First Bridge
You have two paths to build your funnel:

### Path A: The "One-Click" Launch (Recommended)
1.  Go to **Coach Engine > Funnels**.
2.  Scroll to the **Strategic Library**.
3.  Choose your "Vehicle":
    *   **The Consultation:** Best for high-ticket ($3k+) sales.
    *   **The Webinar:** Best for selling to groups at scale.
4.  Click **"Deploy."** The Engine builds every page and setting for you instantly.

### Path B: The "Custom Build"
1.  Click **"+ Add Step"** inside an existing funnel.
2.  Choose your Step Type (Opt-in, Booking, or Checkout).
3.  Reorder them like building blocks to match your specific sales process.

---

## 🛠 2. What's Inside the Box? (Step Types)
You don't need to write code. You just need to choose the right tool for the job:

1.  **The Magnet (Opt-in):** Captures names and emails. It’s the "Front Door" of your business.
2.  **The Calendar (Booking):** A dynamic scheduler that also asks qualifying questions. This ensures you only talk to "A-Player" prospects.
3.  **The Cashier (Checkout):** A frictionless way to accept high-ticket payments via Stripe or PayPal. No more chasing invoices.
4.  **The Handshake (Thank You):** Confirms the action and tells them what to do next.

---

## 📝 3. Your 10-Minute Setup Roadmap
Follow this exactly to get your first lead today:

1.  **Step 1: Define the Prize.** Create a simple offer in the **Clients** tab (even if it's a $0 Strategy Session).
2.  **Step 2: Pick the Vehicle.** Deploy the "Consultation" template in the **Funnels** tab.
3.  **Step 3: Go Live.** Create a new WordPress page, paste the shortcode, and publish.
4.  **Step 4: The Quality Check.** Go to **Bookings** and add one question: *"Why are you the perfect fit for this program?"*
5.  **Step 5: The Test Run.** Open the page yourself. Sign up. Make sure you show up in your own CRM.

---

## 💡 4. How to Read Your Funnel's "Mind"
The Funnel Engine tracks every move.

*   **The Waterfall:** Look at the "Visits vs. Conversions" in your steps.
*   **The Bottleneck:** If 100 people see your "Opt-in" but only 2 sign up, your headline is failing.
*   **The Gold Mine:** If people are booking calls but not paying, your "Offer" needs more value.

**Remember:** You are one funnel away from the business you've always wanted. Let the Engine do the work.
